# C03 — Battle State, Snapshots, and Decisions

Priority: P0  
Status: C03 complete under focused validation
Required order: C03A, then C03B

## Objective

Create the single authoritative mutable battle state and expose only immutable,
versioned information to selectors and future consumers. This package stores
mechanic state but does not yet implement full action resolution.

## Ownership

C03 owns battle-state construction, validation, snapshot projection, pending
decision state, and focused tests. It must not implement presentation, Data
Table loading, strategic AI, damage modifiers, condition triggers, switching
effects, progression, or persistent inventory/storage writes.

## C03A — Internal State and Invariants

Model these internal records:

- Battle settings snapshot, encounter kind, format, current turn, phase, and
  outcome.
- Trainer ownership, side membership, control/selector ID, finite Bag snapshot,
  action allowance, and six party slots.
- Frozen standard-obedience facts required by B00B, such as Trainer/original-
  ownership relationship, obedience threshold/badge snapshot, received/met-level
  facts, and per-battler applicability. Core consumes these facts but does not
  own badge/progression state.
- Battler identity, opaque source Pokemon ID, species/form, level, permanent
  calculated stats, current/max HP, major status, stages, volatiles, Ability,
  original/current held item, and move slots with current/max PP.
- Two active positions per side plus reserves. Single formats leave right slots
  structurally present but unavailable.
- Field, side, hazard, room, weather, terrain, duration, layer, and trigger
  storage using typed IDs; C07 supplies behavior.
- Pending captures, one-success reinforcement state, escape-attempt count,
  capture-capacity snapshot, and end-of-battle item ownership facts.
- Default-disabled per-species/per-encounter wild-flee policies with authored
  trigger, eligibility, and optional probability data.
- Locked actions, pending decision, action/resolution counters, RNG trace, and
  ordered events.

Invariants:

- Maximum party size is six and maximum active count is two per side.
- A battler appears in exactly one party slot and at most one active position.
- Active positions cannot reference Eggs, captured/removed battlers, invalid
  party slots, or fainted battlers except while completing the deterministic
  faint transition.
- Current HP is `0..MaxHP`; HP zero and faint state cannot disagree.
- Current PP is `0..MaxPP`; stages remain `-6..+6`.
- Trainer-owned resources never become negative.
- Terminal outcome and non-terminal phase cannot coexist.

## C03B — Snapshot and Decision Projection

`FBattleSnapshot` contains:

- State version, battle/turn/phase/outcome values and frozen settings ID.
- Trainers, parties, active slots, level, current/max HP, faint state, major
  status, public Ability/item knowledge, move PP visible to that observer, and
  stat stages.
- Weather, terrain, rooms, field effects, side conditions, hazard layers, and
  remaining duration.
- Pending decision identity and legal command/move/switch/item/target options.
- Typed effectiveness knowledge values: Unknown, NotApplicable, Neutral,
  NotVeryEffective, SuperEffective, Immune, or Varies. Never display strings.

Decision generation:

- `FBattleDecisionRequest` names one decision owner and one exact state version.
- Legal options are generated by the core. Selectors never invent raw IDs.
- Every unavailable option has a typed reason when the caller needs to display
  it; hidden information is not exposed through the reason.
- An illegal/stale response returns a rejection and leaves state/RNG unchanged.

Decision-request sequence:

1. Request every human-controlled player Trainer action first, in stable Trainer
   order and then Left/Right active-position order.
2. Request partner-AI actions after the player's selections; its filtered
   observation may include those selections.
3. Request enemy actions after player-side selection, but build their selector
   observations from the filtered pre-choice state so none of the player's
   unexecuted choices are visible.
4. A selector controlling two active positions may return its two choices as one
   validated batch; within the batch positions are ordered Left then Right.
5. If the player controls both player and partner Trainers, collect the player's
   own Trainer first, then the partner Trainer, each Left then Right.

Visibility:

- Enemy selector observations omit the player's current unexecuted choice.
- Partner selector observations may include the player's already selected
  action.
- Unrevealed opponent Abilities/items and unrelated private Bag/party data stay
  hidden.
- Core can expose a public-reveal event without owning messages or UI.

Progression seam:

- Emit a typed opponent-removal checkpoint only after linked effects and faint
  checks.
- `TryApplyBetweenActionsStatRefresh` accepts a matching checkpoint/version,
  participant ID, new supplied level/stats, and Max-HP/current-HP adjustment.
- It is allowed only between actions, cannot alter the locked action order, and
  does not calculate why the stats changed.
- Core emits raw participation, switch, removal, and outcome facts. It does not
  calculate EXP, EV awards, levels, moves, evolution, or rewards.

## Tests

- Valid and invalid Single, Double, and PartnerDouble setups.
- Duplicate battler/party/active IDs and more-than-six/more-than-two rejection.
- HP, PP, stage, resource, and outcome invariant enforcement.
- Snapshot is a deep immutable projection, not a mutable reference to state.
- Observer-specific visibility and no leakage of hidden Ability/item/choice.
- Exact human, partner-selector, and enemy-selector request sequence for Single,
  Double, and PartnerDouble control modes.
- Legal-option generation contains only IDs present in the frozen catalog and
  current state.
- Stale decision and stat-refresh checkpoint rejection.
- A valid stat refresh updates the next action's stats but leaves locked order
  and previous events untouched.

## Acceptance

- Battle state can be created for one valid Single, Double, and PartnerDouble
  fixture without World, Actor, Blueprint, UI, or Data Table access.
- Every invalid setup fails atomically with typed diagnostics.
- Snapshots are deterministic and versioned.
- C04 and C05 can separately consume state queries without directly mutating
  state internals.
